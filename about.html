Our story

About Vantyi & Sons

A family-owned funeral parlour built on compassion, dignity and service to the community.

Our history

Vantyi & Sons Funeral Parlour was established in 1978 in Port Elizabeth, now known as Gqeberha, by Mr. Vantyi.

His vision was to provide compassionate, dignified and affordable funeral services to families during their most difficult times. The parlour's proposed website is intended to make information about its services easier for families to access.

Our commitment

Respect for every family

We aim to communicate with warmth, professionalism and sensitivity, while helping families find the information and support they need.

Our mission

To provide compassionate, professional, and affordable funeral services that honor every life with dignity while supporting families through every step of their journey.

Our vision

To remain one of the Eastern Cape's most trusted and respected family-owned funeral service providers, recognized for excellence, compassion, and community service.
