Welcome to Vantyi & Sons

Honouring every life with dignity

Established in 1978 in Port Elizabeth, now known as Gqeberha, Vantyi & Sons was founded with a vision of providing compassionate, dignified and affordable funeral services.

We understand that arranging a funeral can be an emotional and difficult experience. Our aim is to make important information easy to find and to help families understand the support available to them.
