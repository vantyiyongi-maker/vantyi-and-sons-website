Funeral policy enquiries

Vantyi & Sons' website proposal identifies funeral policies as one of the services families should be able to learn about online.

If you are interested in funeral policy information, use the enquiry form to request details from the parlour. A team member can provide the current information, eligibility requirements, premiums, benefits, exclusions and applicable terms.

Important: This website does not state policy prices, benefits or eligibility rules because these details were not supplied in the project proposal. Please request the official policy documentation before making a decision.

Request information

Send your contact details and indicate that your enquiry relates to funeral policies.

Make a policy enquiry

What to ask about

  • Who may be covered under the policy?
  • What benefits are included?
  • What are the premiums and payment arrangements?
  • Are there waiting periods or exclusions?
  • What documents are needed to submit a claim?
  • Where can the full policy terms and conditions be reviewed?
